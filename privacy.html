Privacy Policy

JM Assistant is a personal tool used by its developer only. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission it reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar events, and may read sender and subject lines of the owner's own Gmail messages.

How data is used

Data is used only to remind the owner about tasks and schedule. It is stored on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
